We investigate a pursuit-evasion game in which the pursuer has a limited sensing range, while the evader has access to global state information. Due to this information asymmetry, our strategy uses the observed relative position to adjust the pursuit heading. The strategy keeps the evader within the sensing range under the stated speed condition; however, finite-time capture is not established. Simulation results illustrate sensing retention in the tested cases, which does not imply a universal capture guarantee.
