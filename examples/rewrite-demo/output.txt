A pursuit-evasion game under asymmetric information is investigated. The pursuer has a limited sensing range, but the evader has access to global state information. To accommodate this information asymmetry, a pursuit strategy is developed to adjust the heading using the observed relative position. Under the stated speed condition, the evader is kept within the sensing range. Finite-time capture is not established. Simulation results illustrate sensing retention in the tested cases and do not establish a universal capture guarantee.
